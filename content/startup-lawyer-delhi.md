### What Delhi founders usually need

- **Company set-up and founder terms.** Private limited company or LLP, founders' agreements with vesting, IP assignment and DPIIT recognition. Companies registered in Delhi file with the Registrar of Companies, NCT of Delhi and Haryana.

- **Fundraising.** Term sheets, share subscription and shareholders' agreements, ESOP schemes, and the Companies Act and FEMA filings after a round.

- **Commercial contracts.** Customer, vendor, SaaS, distribution, franchise and payment-gateway agreements.

- **Compliance.** The DPDP Act, 2023, the four Labour Codes and the Delhi Shops and Establishments Act, gig-worker arrangements, consumer protection and e-commerce rules, and FSSAI for food and supplement brands.

- **Notices and recovery.** Replies to legal notices, cheque-bounce complaints under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, MSME delayed-payment claims, arbitration and recovery suits.

### Forums in Delhi

Delhi matters can run before the district courts and commercial courts in Delhi, the Delhi High Court (including its commercial division and arbitration petitions), the New Delhi benches of the National Company Law Tribunal, the Debts Recovery Tribunals in Delhi and the consumer commissions. Where a matter needs regular appearances, the forum, likely timeline and cost are set out at the start.
