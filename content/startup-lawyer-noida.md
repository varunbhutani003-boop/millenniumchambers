### What Noida founders usually need

- **Company set-up and founder terms.** Private limited company or LLP, founders' agreements, IP assignment and DPIIT recognition. Companies with their registered office in Uttar Pradesh file with the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur.

- **Fundraising.** Term sheets, shareholders' agreements, ESOP schemes and post-round filings.

- **Gig and service-platform compliance.** The four Labour Codes, social security for gig and platform workers, partner agreements, and escalation and safety processes for on-ground service partners.

- **Consumer-facing businesses.** Consumer protection and e-commerce rules, replies to consumer notices and complaints, and FSSAI for food and supplement brands.

- **Offices and leases.** Leases for office space in Noida's IT and commercial sectors, including lock-in, deposit and exit terms.

- **Notices and recovery.** Legal notices, cheque-bounce complaints, MSME delayed-payment claims and arbitration.

### Forums for Noida matters

Noida falls in Gautam Buddh Nagar district, Uttar Pradesh. Depending on the matter, disputes may go to the district and commercial courts there, the Allahabad High Court, the National Company Law Tribunal bench with jurisdiction over Uttar Pradesh, the consumer commissions, or the forum named in the contract's arbitration or jurisdiction clause. Real estate complaints about projects in Noida and Greater Noida go to the Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority.
