# Varun Bhutani — Advocate, Gurugram

## Advocate, Corporate Lawyer and Startup Legal Advisor in Gurugram (Gurgaon)

Varun Bhutani is an advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Delhi (since December 2018) and the Independent Partner of Millennium Chambers, with his office at 325H, Sector 38, Gurugram (Gurgaon), Haryana. He began in litigation before the Delhi High Court and spent nearly seven years since 2019 as an in-house lawyer with India's high-growth technology companies, most recently as Head of Legal at YesMadam.

### Professional background

- Head of Legal, YesMadam Technologies (2026)

- Head of Legal, Zypp Electric (2025–2026) — drafted the organisation's Road Safety SOP manual for its EV fleet

- Chief Manager (Legal), Ecom Express (2024–2025)

- Legal team, OfBusiness / Oxyzo Financial Services (2019–2024) — a founding member of the NBFC's legal team, from incorporation to systemically important NBFC and unicorn

- Associate Advocate, Pavan Duggal Associates, Delhi High Court (2018–2019)

### Areas of work

Startup and venture-capital transactions, founders' and shareholders' agreements, ESOPs, fintech and NBFC regulation, the DPDP Act 2023, the four Labour Codes and gig-worker frameworks, consumer protection, IBC and SARFAESI, corporate governance, trademarks, commercial contracts and commercial real estate.

### Focus: data protection for startups

Varun advises startups on India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, including privacy notices, consent flows, vendor contracts and breach-response planning ahead of the core obligations that apply from 13 May 2027. Read his guide: [DPDP Act compliance for startups in India](/dpdp-act-compliance-startups-india).

### Focus: fundraising and investment documents

Varun works with founders on the documents behind a funding round: term sheets, share subscription and shareholders' agreements, founders' agreements and ESOP schemes, together with the Companies Act and FEMA steps that follow. Read his guide: [term sheets for startups in India](/term-sheet-startups-india).

### Focus: commercial contracts and payment recovery

Much of Varun's in-house work has been on the commercial side of fast-growing companies: vendor and customer agreements, franchise and dealer networks, payment-gateway and logistics contracts, and the notices and recoveries that follow when contracts break down. At OfBusiness and Oxyzo he worked on lending and recovery documentation from the NBFC's incorporation onwards, including matters under the IBC and SARFAESI. At Zypp Electric and Ecom Express he handled EV-fleet, delivery-partner and two-wheeler dealership arrangements, along with accident and on-ground escalations. For founders, this covers drafting contracts that are easier to enforce, replying to legal notices, and using the MSMED Act's 45-day payment rule and the MSE Facilitation Council when a micro or small supplier is not paid. Read his guide: [MSME payment recovery under the MSMED Act](/msme-delayed-payment-recovery-msmed-act).
