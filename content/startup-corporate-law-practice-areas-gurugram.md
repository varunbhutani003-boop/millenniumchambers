# Practice Areas

## Startup, corporate and regulatory law: areas of practice in Gurugram

Millennium Chambers is a Gurugram-based practice that works with founders, startups and growing companies across India. The areas below describe the kind of matters handled; they are listed for information only.

### Startups and venture capital

Company incorporation and structuring, founders' agreements, shareholders' agreements, ESOP schemes, term sheets, seed and Series A investment documentation, due diligence support, FEMA and RBI filings for foreign investment, and post-closing secretarial compliance.

### Commercial contracts

Drafting and review of vendor, customer, SaaS, master services, payment-gateway, franchise, distribution, NDA and partnership agreements, with a focus on clear risk allocation for early-stage businesses.

### Debt recovery, insolvency and disputes

Recovery of loans and unpaid commercial dues for lenders, NBFCs and businesses: legal notices, cheque-bounce complaints under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, enforcement under the SARFAESI Act, applications under Sections 7 and 9 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code before the NCLT, liquidation-stage claims, arbitration, summary and commercial suits, MSME Facilitation Council claims, consumer complaints, injunctions and police or EOW complaints. See [debt recovery lawyer in Gurgaon](/debt-recovery-lawyer-gurgaon).

### NBFC, fintech and lending

Loan agreements, sanction letters and term sheets, digital lending and bill-discounting documentation, co-lending and lending-service-provider arrangements, RBI directions for NBFCs, KYC and AML norms, the fair practices code and RBI outsourcing safeguards in technology and vendor contracts. See [NBFC and fintech lawyer in Gurgaon](/nbfc-fintech-lawyer-gurgaon).

### Debt fundraising and structured finance

Non-convertible debenture issuances, term loans, venture debt, operating and finance leases and asset financing with OEMs and financiers, security and guarantee documents, and the SEBI, RBI and Companies Act compliance that goes with them.

### IPO readiness and corporate governance

Pre-IPO clean-up, review of DRHP sections and legal disclosures alongside merchant bankers and issue counsel, board and committee resolutions, shareholder matters, delegation-of-authority matrices, legal SOPs and periodic legal audits.

### Data protection and technology (DPDP Act, 2023)

Readiness for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025: data mapping, consent notices, privacy policies, platform terms of use, breach-response procedures and vendor data-processing terms.

### Labour codes and the gig workforce

Compliance under the four Labour Codes, gig and platform-worker arrangements, contractor and employment documentation, POSH policies and HR policies for fast-growing teams.

### Consumer protection and e-commerce

E-commerce rules compliance, consumer notices and complaints, advertising and dark-pattern guidelines, refund and grievance-redressal policies.

### Food, nutraceutical and FSSAI regulatory

Regulatory work for food, health-supplement and nutraceutical brands under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006: label and pack reviews against the FSSAI labelling and display regulations, mapping health and nutrient claims to those permitted under the Nutraceuticals and Advertising & Claims regulations, "natural", "100%" and logo-use checks across a product range, product approval routes for non-specified claims, replies to FSSAI notices and improvement notices, and claims review for e-commerce listings and advertising in line with ASCI and CCPA guidance.

### Fleet, logistics and road-accident matters

Road-safety SOPs for rider and delivery fleets, accident-escalation protocols, FIR and police liaison, group personal-accident and motor-insurance claims, Motor Accident Claims Tribunal (MACT) and third-party compensation claims, and indemnity and liability clauses in transporter, fleet-vendor and delivery-partner agreements.

### ESG, EPR and workplace compliance

Extended Producer Responsibility under the Plastic Waste Management Rules, CSR obligations, POSH policies and internal committees, and ethics and compliance frameworks.

### Intellectual property

Trademark searches, filing, prosecution and opposition, brand protection for startups, and IP assignment and licensing clauses in founder and employee agreements.

### Real estate and leasing

Commercial office leases, leave-and-licence agreements, co-working arrangements, title due diligence for warehouse and hub acquisitions, and property documentation review in Gurugram and other Indian cities.

RERA matters under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016: complaints, replies and representation before the Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HRERA Gurugram and Panchkula), its Adjudicating Officers and the Haryana Real Estate Appellate Tribunal.
