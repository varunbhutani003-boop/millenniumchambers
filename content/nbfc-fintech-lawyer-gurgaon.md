### Questions lenders and fintech founders ask

**Does a fintech startup need an NBFC licence to lend?**
Lending as a business from your own balance sheet needs an RBI registration. Many fintech startups instead work as a lending-service provider to a bank or NBFC, where the regulated lender makes the loan and the platform sources, services or collects under an agreement that follows the RBI's digital lending rules.

**What does a lender check before signing with a fintech platform?**
Who holds the customer relationship and the data, how money moves between borrower and lender, what the platform may say to borrowers, how complaints and collections are handled, and what happens to the loan book if the arrangement ends.

**Can the practice act as outside legal counsel for an NBFC that has no in-house lawyer?**
Yes. Work is taken on a monthly retainer or matter by matter. See [legal retainer for startups and growing companies](/legal-retainer-outside-counsel-startups).

**Does the practice handle recovery for NBFCs outside Gurgaon?**
Notices, arbitration and documentation are handled for lenders across India. Court and tribunal work is taken in Gurugram and Delhi NCR, and matters elsewhere are run with local counsel.
