# Legal Retainer for Startups and Growing Companies

## An outside legal team for companies without an in-house lawyer

Most companies need a lawyer every week long before they can justify hiring one. Contracts arrive, a customer sends a notice, an investor asks for documents, a new law changes how the team must work. Millennium Chambers, the practice of Advocate Varun Bhutani in Gurugram (Gurgaon), takes this work on a monthly retainer and acts as the company's outside legal team.

Varun was Head of Legal at YesMadam, where he led a team of five, and at Zypp Electric, and earlier built legal processes at Ecom Express and at Oxyzo, the NBFC arm of OfBusiness. The retainer is that in-house experience offered from outside: one lawyer who knows the business, its contracts and its history, instead of a new brief to a new firm each time.

### What a retainer usually covers

- **Contracts.** Drafting, review and negotiation of customer, vendor, SaaS, partnership, franchise and lease agreements, and standard templates the team can use without a lawyer each time.

- **Notices and disputes.** Replies to legal notices and consumer complaints, demand notices for unpaid dues, and coordination of any litigation or arbitration.

- **Compliance.** The DPDP Act, 2023, the four Labour Codes, POSH, consumer protection and e-commerce rules, and sector rules such as RBI norms for lenders or FSSAI rules for food brands.

- **People.** Employment and consultant agreements, ESOP documents, policies, and exits handled without later claims.

- **Founders and investors.** Board and shareholder resolutions, support in fundraising and due diligence, and investor reporting obligations.

- **Brand.** Trademark filings, examination replies and oppositions.

### How it works

The first month starts with a short legal review of the company's contracts, registrations and open disputes, so that the urgent gaps are known. After that, requests come by email, WhatsApp or a shared tracker, with an agreed turnaround. Large one-off projects, such as a funding round or a court case, are scoped separately so that the monthly arrangement stays predictable.

### Who it suits

Seed to Series B startups, family-run and mid-sized companies, NBFCs and fintech companies without a legal team, and foreign companies starting operations in India who need a single point of contact on the ground.

### Questions companies ask

**What is the difference between a legal retainer and hiring a lawyer per matter?**
Per-matter work suits a single transaction or dispute. A retainer suits a company with steady legal work, because the lawyer already knows the business and nothing waits for a fresh engagement each time.

**When does a company need a full-time in-house lawyer instead?**
When contract volume, regulatory filings or litigation fill a working week. Until then a retainer usually costs less and gives access to more senior experience.

**Can the retainer work alongside an existing law firm or company secretary?**
Yes. The retainer often manages outside firms on specialised matters and works with the company secretary and chartered accountant on filings.

**Is the work limited to companies in Gurgaon?**
No. Advisory and contract work is done remotely for companies across India. Court and tribunal work is taken in Gurugram and Delhi NCR.
