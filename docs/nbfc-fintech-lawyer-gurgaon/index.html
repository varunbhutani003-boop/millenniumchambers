NBFC and Fintech Lawyer in Gurgaon (Gurugram)

Legal support for NBFCs, digital lenders and fintech companies

Millennium Chambers, the practice of Advocate Varun Bhutani at 325H, Sector 38, Gurugram (Gurgaon), works with NBFCs, lending-service providers and fintech startups on documentation, RBI compliance and recovery. Varun joined the legal team of Oxyzo Financial Services, the NBFC arm of OfBusiness, at its incorporation and stayed for over four years as it grew into a systemically important NBFC. He later ran debt fundraising at Zypp Electric and negotiated lending-partner arrangements for a consumer platform at YesMadam, so he has sat on the lender's side, the borrower's side and the platform's side of the same documents.

Lending documentation

  • Loan agreements, sanction letters and term sheets for secured and unsecured business loans

  • Supply-chain finance, invoice and digital bill-discounting documentation

  • Security documents: hypothecation, pledge, mortgage, personal and corporate guarantees

  • Key fact statements, borrower-facing terms and other customer documents

  • Co-lending, lending-service-provider and direct-selling-agent agreements between lenders and platforms

RBI and regulatory compliance

  • The RBI framework for NBFCs, including the fair practices code and grievance-redressal requirements

  • KYC and anti-money-laundering norms and customer onboarding journeys

  • Digital lending rules: disclosures, fund flows, data handling and the roles of the lender and the platform

  • RBI outsourcing safeguards in technology, collection-agency and other vendor contracts

  • Data protection under the DPDP Act, 2023 for lenders and fintech apps

  • Policy documents, board notes and audit-ready lending records

Raising debt

Non-convertible debenture issuances, term loans, venture debt, and operating and finance leases, with the SEBI, RBI and Companies Act compliance that goes with them. See the practice areas page.

Recovery

Demand notices, Section 138 cheque-bounce complaints, arbitration, SARFAESI enforcement for eligible lenders, insolvency applications before the NCLT, civil recovery suits and police or EOW complaints. See debt recovery lawyer in Gurgaon.

Questions lenders and fintech founders ask

Does a fintech startup need an NBFC licence to lend? Lending as a business from your own balance sheet needs an RBI registration. Many fintech startups instead work as a lending-service provider to a bank or NBFC, where the regulated lender makes the loan and the platform sources, services or collects under an agreement that follows the RBI's digital lending rules.

What does a lender check before signing with a fintech platform? Who holds the customer relationship and the data, how money moves between borrower and lender, what the platform may say to borrowers, how complaints and collections are handled, and what happens to the loan book if the arrangement ends.

Can the practice act as outside legal counsel for an NBFC that has no in-house lawyer? Yes. Work is taken on a monthly retainer or matter by matter. See legal retainer for startups and growing companies.

Does the practice handle recovery for NBFCs outside Gurgaon? Notices, arbitration and documentation are handled for lenders across India. Court and tribunal work is taken in Gurugram and Delhi NCR, and matters elsewhere are run with local counsel.
