Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Millennium Chambers ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data through www.millenniumchambers.in, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Information We Collect

You can browse this website without giving us any personal data. We collect only the information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, phone number, the details of your query and, for internship applicants, your CV. Our website host, Google, may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and cookies, under Google's own privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, carry out conflict checks, evaluate internship applications and meet our legal and professional obligations. By sending us your information, you consent to its use for these purposes. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use it for marketing without your consent.

Sharing, Retention and Security

We share personal data only with service providers we rely on to run this website and our email (such as Google), or where required by law, a court or a regulator. We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected; internship applications are deleted within 12 months unless the applicant is engaged. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your Rights and Grievance Redressal

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, update or erase your personal data, withdraw your consent, or nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf. For any such request or grievance, write to our Grievance Officer, Varun Bhutani, at millenniumchambersindia@gmail.com. We will respond within the time prescribed by law. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
