Startup Legal Brief: the DPDP countdown

29 October 2026

The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025 and are coming into force in phases. Rules on the Data Protection Board applied immediately. Rule 4 on the registration and obligations of Consent Managers applies from 13 November 2026. The core obligations (notice and consent, reasonable security safeguards, breach intimation, data retention, verifiable parental consent for children's data, data principal rights, Significant Data Fiduciary duties and cross-border transfers) apply from 13 May 2027.

Points startups commonly review before May 2027: a map of what personal data is collected and why; consent notices in clear language; security safeguards and a breach-response plan; retention and deletion schedules; processes for handling access, correction and erasure requests; age-gating where children may use the product; and data-processing terms with vendors. Penalties under the DPDP Act can go up to Rs 250 crore for a failure to take reasonable security safeguards.
