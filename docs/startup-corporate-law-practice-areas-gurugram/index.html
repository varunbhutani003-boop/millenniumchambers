Practice Areas

Startup, corporate and regulatory law: areas of practice in Gurugram

Millennium Chambers is a Gurugram-based practice that works with founders, startups and growing companies across India. The areas below describe the kind of matters handled; they are listed for information only.

Startups and venture capital

Company incorporation and structuring, founders' agreements, shareholders' agreements, ESOP schemes, term sheets, seed and Series A investment documentation, due diligence support, FEMA and RBI filings for foreign investment, and post-closing secretarial compliance.

Commercial contracts

Drafting and review of vendor, customer, SaaS, master services, payment-gateway, franchise, distribution, NDA and partnership agreements, with a focus on clear risk allocation for early-stage businesses.

Data protection and technology (DPDP Act, 2023)

Readiness for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025: data mapping, consent notices, privacy policies, platform terms of use, breach-response procedures and vendor data-processing terms.

Labour codes and the gig workforce

Compliance under the four Labour Codes, gig and platform-worker arrangements, contractor and employment documentation, POSH policies and HR policies for fast-growing teams.

Consumer protection and e-commerce

E-commerce rules compliance, consumer notices and complaints, advertising and dark-pattern guidelines, refund and grievance-redressal policies.

Food, nutraceutical and FSSAI regulatory

Regulatory work for food, health-supplement and nutraceutical brands under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006: label and pack reviews against the FSSAI labelling and display regulations, mapping health and nutrient claims to those permitted under the Nutraceuticals and Advertising & Claims regulations, "natural", "100%" and logo-use checks across a product range, product approval routes for non-specified claims, replies to FSSAI notices and improvement notices, and claims review for e-commerce listings and advertising in line with ASCI and CCPA guidance.

Intellectual property

Trademark searches, filing, prosecution and opposition, brand protection for startups, and IP assignment and licensing clauses in founder and employee agreements.

Real estate and leasing

Commercial office leases, leave-and-licence agreements, co-working arrangements and property documentation review in Gurugram and other Indian cities.
